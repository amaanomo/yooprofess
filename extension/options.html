2. Wake word

Works out of the box: Nova listens on your Panopto lecture tab while it's open and visible, using Chrome's speech recognition. No key needed. The first time, Chrome asks to allow the microphone for the Panopto site. Click Allow. While it's on, Chrome sends what the mic hears to Google's speech service.
